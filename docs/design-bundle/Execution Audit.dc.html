[ 05 ] THE ONE SYSTEM CHANGE

R9 — one band and one major section per route.

Four rungs. A route may use BAND at most once and MAJOR at most once; everything else is MINOR or QUIET. That single constraint is what turns eight correct pages into a composed site, and it is enforceable by the same kind of gate that already counts marks.

RUNG TREATMENT WHERE IT IS ALLOWED
BAND Full-bleed, #f0f1f3, rules top and bottom, figures at 92px / 40px on mobile home proof · colophon numbers · the write-up finding
MAJOR 900 or 1152 wide, 32px heading, 48px of air above, tick rule the one section the reader came for — the index table, the bench, the record
MINOR 680 wide, 22px heading, 32px above, no tick rule everything supporting — stack table, rights, toolbox
QUIET 680 wide, no heading at all — a 12px mono label and the content provenance, languages, footnotes, counted-from lines

Applied today: colophon uses MAJOR three times, About three, privacy six. Under R9 privacy has one MAJOR — the flows — and five QUIET sections, which is also what it means.
